FourShot Privacy Policy

HomeSupport

Last updated: October 17, 2026

FourShot does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The videos you record are processed and saved only on your iPhone, in your Photos library.
  • Face tracking for beauty effects runs entirely on your iPhone. No image or face data ever leaves the device.
  • Your settings (looks, filters, framing) are stored on your iPhone only.
  • FourShot has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and does not send anything over the internet.

Purchases

FourShot Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and keeps the purchase record for your Apple ID; FourShot only asks the App Store whether Pro is unlocked. FourShot never sees your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Permissions

  • Camera: to record your scene and your reaction at the same time.
  • Microphone: to record sound with your videos.
  • Photos: to save your recorded videos (and, if you turn on Album per take, to put each take in its own album).

You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Children

FourShot is a general camera app rated 4+. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
